Cerchio is a digital watch face: the time, large and centered, inside a
24-hour dial.

**The circle** follows the sun: amber by day, orange through dawn and dusk,
sky blue at night, with a small mark at solar noon. A line on it shows the
hour, and a seconds hand sweeps the edge of the screen, in low power mode too
where the watch allows it.

**Above the time**, battery, phone, alarm and AM/PM, shown only when there is
something to report. **Below it**, one data field of your choice from 26
built-in complications.

- Set on the watch in Garmin's watch face editor: data field, a color for the
  numerals and one for the seconds hand
- Always-on mode shows just the time
