Cerchio is a digital watch face: the time, large and centered, inside a
24-hour dial.

**The dial** follows the sun: the circle is amber by day, orange through
dawn and dusk, sky blue at night, with a small mark at solar noon. A line
on the circle shows the hour, and the seconds go round the edge of the
screen, in low power mode too where the watch allows it.

**Above the time**, battery, phone, alarm, recovery time, amber past a
day and orange past two, the wind as a dot on a compass
ring where it blows, amber when moderate and orange when strong, and AM/PM,
shown only when there is something to report. **Below it**, two data
fields of your choice, each an icon and its value: the weather as an icon
for the conditions beside the temperature, and the sun as its next sunset
or sunrise, its icon orange through dawn and dusk.

- A goal dot glides round from the top as your steps goal is done, a ring
  until it is done and solid once it is
- Set on the watch in Garmin's watch face editor: data fields, a color
  for the time and data, and an accent for the seconds and the goal dot
- Always-on mode shows just the time
