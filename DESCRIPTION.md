Cerchio is a digital watch face: the time, large and centered, inside a
24-hour dial.

**The dial** follows the sun: the circle is amber by day, orange through
dawn and dusk, sky blue at night, with a small mark at solar noon. A line
on the circle shows the hour, and a seconds hand sweeps the edge of the
screen, in low power mode too where the watch allows it.

**Above the time**, battery, phone, alarm, wind or messages, and AM/PM, shown
only when there is something to report; the wind arrow turns orange, then
red, as it picks up. **Below it**, one data field of your choice from 26
built-in complications, and under that a sun that counts down the last hour
before sunrise or sunset, glows orange through twilight and marks solar noon.

- A goal dot glides round from the top as your steps, floors or intensity
  minutes goal is done
- Numerals style: gray hour numerals round the circle, the numeral of
  the recovery hours left larger and in the accent color; past a day the
  24, orange up to two days and red beyond; the goal dot a half dot at the
  edge of the glass
- Plain style: no numerals, the circle out against the edge of the glass
  with a wider seconds hand and a whole goal dot just inside it
- Set on the watch in Garmin's watch face editor: style, data field, goal,
  wind or messages, a color for the time and data, and an accent for the
  seconds hand, goal dot and recovery numeral
- Always-on mode shows just the time
